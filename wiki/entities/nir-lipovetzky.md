# Nir Lipovetzky

Lecturer and coordinator for the first half of COMP90054, and a researcher in automated
planning at the University of Melbourne.

## Key facts

- Completed a PhD in Barcelona in 2012; at the University of Melbourne since 2012.
- Teaches weeks 1 to 6 of the subject, returning in week 11 for an advanced session on current planning research. [[guang-hu]] takes weeks 6 to 12.
- Research spans four areas: planning technology (the LAPKT solver library, planning.domains, Planimation), planning for autonomy in industry, bounded general AI such as playing all Atari games with one algorithm, and the integration of planning, learning and control.
- Planimation, a tool that turns a plan's action sequence into an animated visualisation, began as a student software project in this subject and went on to win a best demonstration award at a major international planning conference.
- Rejects AGI as a research framing on the grounds that an unbounded definition cannot be falsified, and argues for *bounded* general AI, where the class of problems generalised over is stated in advance.
- Teaches through peer instruction: poll-everywhere questions, vote, discuss in pairs, vote again. Explicitly asks to be interrupted rather than deliver a monologue.

## Relevance to AI Planning for Autonomy

Lipovetzky wrote and presents the week 1 material, both the pre-recorded videos and the
two live lectures, and the subject's framing is his: planning as discrete, higher-level
sequential decision making, sitting above continuous control and adjacent to learning;
generality bounded and stated rather than assumed. The assessment design follows from
the same view, using standards-based grading with resubmission so that students have no
incentive to outsource the learning.
