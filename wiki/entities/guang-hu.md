# Guang Hu

Lecturer for the second half of COMP90054, covering probabilistic planning and
reinforcement learning from week 6.

## Key facts

- Completed a PhD at the University of Melbourne on epistemic planning, supervised by [[nir-lipovetzky]] and Tim Miller.
- Epistemic planning concerns reasoning about agents' knowledge of the world and about other agents' knowledge of the world — planning over mental states rather than physical ones.
- Research interests also cover multi-agent systems, reinforcement learning, goal recognition and proactive assistance, and neuro-symbolic and LLM-based reasoning.
- Has supervised a large number of student research projects, which broadened the range of topics he works across.
- Takes the subject from week 6 to week 12, with Lipovetzky returning for an advanced planning week in week 11.

## Relevance to AI Planning for Autonomy

The second half of the subject relaxes classical planning's determinism assumption,
moving to [[markov-decision-process]] models and then to reinforcement learning, where
the transition probabilities are not given and must be learned through interaction. Hu's
own research on epistemic planning is one of the beyond-classical directions named in
week 1a as interesting but outside the syllabus.
