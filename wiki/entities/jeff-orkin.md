# Jeff Orkin

Game AI developer, the AI lead at Monolith Productions on *No One Lives Forever 2*
(2002) and *F.E.A.R.* (2005), where he introduced goal-oriented action planning to
commercial video games.

## Key facts

- Built the enemy AI in *F.E.A.R.* on planning rather than hand-coded behaviour: the world is described in facts and actions, in the manner of [[strips]], and a solver produces the behaviour at run time.
- *F.E.A.R.* is remembered historically as the first commercial game whose AI was generated by a planner rather than scripted, and it surprised players precisely because the behaviour was not hand-authored.
- The technique had to fit an extremely tight computation budget: the AI in a commercial game gets close to zero CPU cycles, since almost everything goes to graphics.
- Wrote the paper describing the approach, which the week 3 lecture recommends reading.

## Relevance to AI Planning for Autonomy

*F.E.A.R.* is used in the subject as evidence for the [[models-and-solvers]] frame
outside its usual settings. The lesson drawn is not that games need planners, but that
describing a world in facts and actions and handing it to a general solver produced
better behaviour than hand-coding it, under a computation budget far tighter than a
robotics or logistics application would face.
