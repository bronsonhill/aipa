# Jörg Hoffmann

Author of the widely recommended survey paper "Everything You Always Wanted to Know
About Planning (But Were Afraid to Ask)", cited in the subject as an accessible
introduction to what planning research is and why it matters.

## Key facts

- The lecturer specifically recommends the first, non-technical part of the paper as a way to understand the field's motivation and open questions, and describes the author as a leading contributor to planning research.

## Relevance to AI Planning for Autonomy

Named as recommended supplementary reading alongside the week 4 material on
computational approaches to planning, and again as author of the FF paper — the
JAIR Best Paper Award winner that introduced the [[relaxed-plan-heuristic]]
($h^\text{FF}$), helpful actions, and enforced hill-climbing.
